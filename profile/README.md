# Quesler

**Digital studio in Los Cabos, B.C.S., Mexico.**
We help local businesses and entrepreneurs build their digital side, even if they don't have an online presence yet. Projects of any size are welcome.

## What we do

- **Custom software** — websites, online stores, iOS/Android apps, internal systems, integrations and AI automations.
- **Content** — product and space photography, video and aerial shots, social media, campaigns and ads.
- **Brand** — logo, visual identity, brand guidelines, stationery and packaging.

## How we build

We like simple, maintainable stacks. Our own website is built with Next.js (App Router), TypeScript, Tailwind CSS and shadcn/ui, with bilingual (es/en) content.
